Privacy Policy

Effective Date: August 31, 2026 Last Updated: August 31, 2026

1. Introduction

1.1 This Privacy Policy describes how the operator of momentumlabs.co.in (the "Website"), currently an individual founder operating as Momentum Labs pending incorporation ("we," "us," "our"), collects, uses, discloses, and protects personal information ("Personal Data") in connection with your use of the Website.

1.2 This Policy applies to the Website only. The Akira desktop application (including its beta version) is governed by a separate, dedicated privacy policy provided to you at the time of installation.

1.3 By using the Website, you consent to the data practices described in this Policy.

2. Personal Data We Collect

2.1 Waitlist Registration. When you join the private beta waitlist, we collect your email address and the timestamp of registration.

2.2 Inquiries and Communications. When you reach out to us via email or contact forms (e.g., to hello@momentumlabs.co.in or founder@momentumlabs.co.in), we collect your email address, name (if provided), and the content of your message.

2.3 Future Account Data. Upon introduction of user accounts for product access and subscription management, this Policy will be updated in advance to disclose the additional Personal Data collected at that time.

2.4 Cookies & Tracking. We do not currently deploy analytics, tracking, or advertising cookies on the Website. Any future deployment will be disclosed in an updated version of this Policy prior to activation.

2.5 Server Logs. When you access the Website, our hosting provider (Vercel) automatically processes standard technical request data (such as IP address, browser type, and access timestamps) for routing, security, and DDoS mitigation. We do not use this data to identify or profile individuals.

3. Legal Basis and Purpose of Processing

3.1 We process your Personal Data for the following purposes:

  • (a) to administer the waitlist and notify you regarding Beta Program access;
  • (b) to respond to inquiries submitted via email or contact links;
  • (c) to send service-related communications.

3.2 Where applicable law requires a legal basis for processing (e.g., under the EU General Data Protection Regulation or India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023), our basis is your consent, given by voluntarily submitting your Personal Data through the Website.

3.3 We do not sell Personal Data, and we do not use it for third-party advertising.

5. Data Retention

5.1 Personal Data collected via the Website is retained until (a) you request its deletion, or (b) it is no longer necessary for the purpose for which it was collected, whichever occurs first.

6. Your Rights

6.1 Subject to applicable law, you may request to:

  • (a) obtain confirmation of, and access to, Personal Data we hold about you;
  • (b) correct inaccurate Personal Data;
  • (c) request deletion of your Personal Data;
  • (d) withdraw consent, including removal from the Waitlist, at any time.

6.2 To exercise these rights, contact hello@momentumlabs.co.in. We will respond within a reasonable timeframe, and in any event within the period required by applicable law.

6.3 If you are located in the European Economic Area, you additionally have the right to lodge a complaint with your local supervisory authority. If you are located in India, you have rights under the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023, including the right to grievance redressal as described in Section 9.

7.1 We implement reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect Personal Data, including reliance on the security infrastructure of our third-party processors. No method of electronic storage or transmission is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

8. Children's Privacy

8.1 The Website is not directed at, and we do not knowingly collect Personal Data from, individuals under the age of 13 (or the applicable minimum age in your jurisdiction).
