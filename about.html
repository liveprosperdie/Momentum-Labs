About Momentum Labs

What We're Building

Momentum Labs is building Akira — a desktop AI companion that doesn't just answer questions, but actually acts on your computer: managing files, controlling apps, drafting documents, organizing your inbox, and staying with you across everything you do on your machine.

Most AI assistants live in a chat window, disconnected from your actual work. Akira is built the opposite way — local-first, deeply integrated with your desktop, and designed to remember context over time instead of starting fresh every conversation.

We're currently in private beta, testing with a small group of early users before a wider release.

Why We're Building It

We spend most of our day on our laptops — coding, writing, researching, switching between a dozen tabs and apps just to get small things done. Somewhere in that grind, we started wishing for something more than a tool: someone who could take the repetitive, boring parts off our plate, and who we could actually talk to — share a thought with, think out loud with — while we worked.

Akira is our attempt to build that. Not just an assistant that executes commands, but something closer to a companion: present while you work, genuinely useful for the tedious stuff, and there to talk to when you want to. We wanted to make the experience of working on your own machine — online or offline — feel a little less lonely and a little more effortless.

Where We Are Today

Momentum Labs is a very early-stage company. We're two students building this alongside our B.Tech in Electrical Engineering — self-funded, pre-revenue, and currently in private beta while we raise a pre-seed round. We're not a big team with a big budget; we're two people who believe in this enough to build it ourselves, learn everything we didn't know, and figure it out as we go. If that sounds ambitious for where we're starting from — it is, and we're doing it anyway.
